07 · Methodology & limitations

Source: UK Police street-level bicycle theft API (data.police.uk), Open Government Licence v3.0.

Pipeline: Archived JSON → DuckDB raw table → typed/deduplicated staging → fact/dimension tables → analytical marts → standalone HTML.

Geographic coverage: Selected collection points in London and South East England, including Brighton and Guildford. This is not a complete count for London or the South East.

Time: Police data is monthly, not an exact crime timestamp. Dates in the warehouse represent the first day of each reporting month.

Interpretation: Police street coordinates are approximate. Counts are not exposure-adjusted risk measures, and differences may reflect reporting, coverage and activity. Investigation outcomes can change after collection.

Reproducibility: The warehouse and this report are rebuilt from the archived raw responses; no live API connection is required to reproduce the saved dataset.
